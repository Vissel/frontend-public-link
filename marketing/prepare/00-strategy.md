# PublicLink Zero-Cost Organic Marketing Strategy

Master plan for a **0đ / pure-organic** campaign targeting Vietnamese **sellers** (primary) and **buyers**
(secondary). Phased: **awareness -> conversion -> advocacy** across **8 weeks / 56 days** (then ongoing).
No ad spend, no paid tools - only sweat equity (~5-8 hrs/week).

---

## 1. Product & positioning

**What PublicLink is:** the fastest way for a small seller to sell online. In ~30 seconds you create **one
product link + one QR code**. Customers open it, place an order, and **transfer straight into your own bank
account via your own VietQR**. You track orders and export them to Excel. Priced by lightweight plans
(**a plan = a number of days + a number of links**, e.g. **10.000đ / 3 days / 1 link**). Two flows are
featured in this kit: **normal product selling** and **yoga-class booking**.

**One-line message (use everywhere):**
> Tạo 1 link. Nhận đơn / giữ chỗ. Tiền về thẳng tài khoản của bạn. Chỉ 10.000đ / 3 ngày.

**Positioning vs the channels sellers already use:**

| Competitor channel | Their pain | PublicLink answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook / Messenger** | Chasing "còn hàng không?", "STK?", lost in DMs | One link collects orders automatically; no manual reply |
| **Zalo** | Same manual chat + copy-paste STK | Buyer scans QR, transfers to seller's own account |
| **Shopee / TikTok Shop** | Commission per order, funds held, rules/penalties | **Money goes straight to the seller's bank**, no platform middleman on the payment |
| **Building a website** | Costs money + time + tech skill | **No website, no coding** - link in 30s |
| **Excel / paper roster** (yoga, classes) | Manual, double-bookings, no self-serve | Students book themselves via one link; live "Còn lại" count |

**Unique selling points to hammer in every post:**
1. **Tiền về thẳng tài khoản bạn** (own VietQR shown on the page - not held by a platform).
2. **30 giây tạo link** - no website, no coding, no invite.
3. **Phiên bán có đếm ngược** (timed flash-sale sessions create urgency).
4. **Quản lý đơn + xuất Excel** in one place.
5. **Phí cực thấp** theo gói (ngày + số link) - 10.000đ / 3 ngày.
6. Works for **products AND booking services** (yoga classes, courts).

## 2. Audience personas

**Primary - Sellers**
- **Normal-product sellers**: online shop owners, mothers selling food/cosmetics, market stalls, livestream
  sellers, resellers. Pain: drowning in Messenger/Zalo chats, missed orders, copying STK by hand.
- **Class/service sellers (yoga)**: yoga coaches & studios. Pain: roster on Excel/Zalo, double-bookings, no
  self-serve booking, chasing payment per session.
- Mindset: mobile-first, price-sensitive, trust peers/reviews, active in Facebook & Zalo seller groups.

**Secondary - Buyers**
- End customers who open the seller's link/QR. They matter twice: they convert the seller's sale, and they
  *see* PublicLink on the page (powered-by) -> many become sellers. Keep the buyer experience the hero of
  demos ("khách chỉ cần quét mã").

## 3. Channel ranking for $0 organic in Vietnam

Ranked by free-reach potential for this audience:

1. **TikTok** - best free organic reach for short video; huge `#banhangonline` / `#review` / small-business
   community. Primary home for the two shorts.
2. **Facebook** - the largest VN audience. Three levers at $0: **Page**, **Reels**, and **Groups** (seller
   communities are the single biggest free acquisition channel). Plus Marketplace listings.
3. **Zalo** - sellers' daily driver. **Zalo OA** + personal seeding into seller groups/chats.
4. **YouTube Shorts** - repurpose TikTok videos; gives long-tail search life ("cách tạo link bán hàng").
5. **Google Business Profile + SEO** - free listing + long-tail intent keywords ("tạo link bán hàng miễn phí",
   "tạo QR thanh toán đơn hàng").
6. **Instagram Reels** - repurpose for younger sellers / lifestyle food & fashion.

**The built-in viral loop (our strongest $0 lever):** every public sale page is a shareable artifact that
buyers see. Add a "Powered by PublicLink - Tạo link của bạn" CTA on the page (see `setup-checklist.md`).
Each seller's link quietly recruits the next seller. Measure with the referral UTM.

## 5. Phases mapped to the 56 days

- **Phase 0 - Foundation (before Day 1):** all of `prepare/` - channel assets, brand kit, GA4 + UTM, seed
  15-20 groups, produce the two shorts, add the referral CTA. Do this once, ahead of Day 1.
- **Phase 1 - Awareness (Weeks 1-4, Day 1-28):** daily short videos + posts, value-first group seeding,
  hashtags/SEO, reply to every comment. *KPI:* reach, video views, follower growth, **first 30-50
  registrations**.
  - Week 1 (Day 1-7): launch - debut both shorts, one feature demo each day.
  - Week 2 (Day 8-14): pain + education.
  - Week 3 (Day 15-21): feature demos (own-bank QR, countdown, Excel).
  - Week 4 (Day 22-28): comparison + scale (vs Shopee/FB/Zalo), first proof.
- **Phase 2 - Conversion (Weeks 5-7, Day 29-49):** tutorials + strong CTA, **starter promo (10.000đ / 3
  ngày / free trial)**, testimonials, referral push, re-engage every commenter. *KPI:* sign-ups -> **first
  link created** -> **paid plan**.
- **Phase 3 - Advocacy (Week 8, Day 50-56, then ongoing):** UGC, seller spotlights, community/referral
  challenge, recap. *KPI:* referral sign-ups, repeat/retention, content that compounds.

## 6. $0 tooling stack

- **CapCut** (free) - edit both shorts, auto-captions, templates.
- **Canva Free** - thumbnails, quote cards, comparison graphics.
- **Phone/desktop screen recorder** - capture the real app (the "no person" footage).
- **Native analytics** - TikTok/Facebook/Zalo insights + YouTube Studio.
- **Google Analytics 4** (free) - measure link traffic/conversions via UTM.
- **Google Business Profile** - free local + search listing.
- **A link shortener with tracking** (Bitly free tier or your own UTM link) for the referral loop.

---

## 7. KPIs & how to track them for free

| Stage | Metric | Free tool |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Video views, impressions, profile visits | Platform native insights |
| Interest | Link clicks to `share-link.top` | GA4 + UTM (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) |
| Sign-up | New seller accounts | Backend / admin count, weekly |
| Activation | Sellers who create their **first link** | Admin "Đơn hàng" list growth |
| Revenue | Paid plans (10.000đ etc.) | Payment confirmation queue |
| Referral | Sign-ups from `utm_medium=poweredby` | GA4 / UTM |

Weekly: log each day's KPI in the day file; every Sunday note the top post -> turn it into more of that.

## 8. Weekly sweat-equity budget (~5-8 hrs)

- **Batch, don't build daily:** record both videos once in prep; each day is mostly copy-paste + engage.
- Daily (30-60 min): publish the day's main post, do the listed group seeds, reply to 100% of comments/DMs.
- Weekly: 1 Google Business update, review KPI, pick a winner to re-cut.

---

## 9. Rules that keep $0 reach alive

- **Value-first in groups** - help, don't blast; follow each group's self-promo rules.
- **Reply fast** - early comments/DMs are the algorithm signal and the real conversion moment.
- **One idea per post** - single hook, single CTA.
- **Re-cut winners** - when something performs, repost a new hook/opening with the same core.
- **Same spine everywhere** - "Tạo 1 link. Nhận đơn / giữ chỗ." + "Chỉ 10.000đ / 3 ngày".
